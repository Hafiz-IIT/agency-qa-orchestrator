# Research agenda

## Question
**Can explicit requirement coverage and acceptance evidence reduce false-complete delivery in multi-agent software workflows?**

## Hypotheses
### H1
Coverage gates reduce delivery of artifacts that omit requirements.

### H2
Criterion-level verification catches failures that artifact presence alone misses.

### H3
Evidence summaries reduce human review time relative to raw task logs.

## Experiments
1. Generate synthetic projects with controlled missing requirements and compare naive completion vs gated delivery.
2. Introduce correlated verifier mistakes and measure false-ready rate.
3. Compare per-criterion vs per-artifact verification granularity.

## Metrics
- false-ready rate
- coverage recall
- verification burden
- review time
- missed-requirement rate
